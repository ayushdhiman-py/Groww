▶ 💡 Quick Guide to Indicators Click to expand/collapse
Volume & ⚡ Shows how much trading is happening today. A full bar means it's the most traded stock right now. Yellow (or ⚡) means there is an unusual trading spike.
Chart Sparkline showing recent price trends. Click the sparkline to open a larger interactive chart overlaid with EMA 21 & EMA 50.
EMA Gap & Cross 🟣GC or "Cross 21>50 UP" is a Golden Cross (very bullish). "Gap" shows trend strength—a wider gap means stronger momentum.
MACD Trend direction momentum. ▲ Bull means the MACD line is above the signal line (buying pressure). ▼ Bear means selling pressure.
Day & 52W Range The range bar shows where the current price sits between the day's lowest and highest price, and between the 52-week lowest and highest price. A marker near the right = close to the top of its range.
Flags & Score ⚑ Green flags are bullish triggers, Red are bearish. The row of small boxes shows individual signals (e.g. RSI, VWAP). Green = ON.
