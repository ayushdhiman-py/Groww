  [DONE] Not "price+5%" — uses ATR, ORB confirmation, structure, MACD accel, RS,
         sector strength/momentum, prev-day/52W/weekly-high resistance capping,
         distance-already-travelled/exhaustion discount. Returns zone/remaining/
         confidence, explicitly labeled "estimate, not a guarantee."
  [DONE] Confidence label now grounded in backtest.mjs's empirical outcomes.
         backtest.mjs's computeUpsideCalibration() buckets every simulated
         trade by its LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH confidence label and records the real
         hit rate (did MFE actually reach the estimated zoneHighPct?),
         persisted to data/upside_calibration.json after a backtest run.
         entry_score.mjs's applyUpsideCalibration() reads it live (re-checked
         every 5min) and DEMOTES a label when its historical hit rate is
         poor (<40%) with a sufficient sample (>=20), or CONFIRMS it with a
         note when the hit rate is strong (>=70%) — deliberately asymmetric:
         calibration never upgrades a label, only demotes or confirms,
         so it can only ever add caution, never inflate today's rule-based
         read. Falls back to the pure rule-based label with zero adjustment
         when no calibration file exists yet or the sample is too small
         (model-safety fallback). Still does not directly weight raw volume
         (only indirectly via ORB) — minor, not addressed.
